Farm Harvest Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dave Wuz Here ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Farm Harvest ("the Game").

Information We Collect

Farm Harvest does not require players to create an account or provide personal information.

We do not intentionally collect, request, or store personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Postal address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or other files from your device
  • Payment information
  • User account information

Farm Harvest does not contain advertising and does not offer in-app purchases.

The Game is designed to function without requiring players to provide personal information to us.

Device and Usage Information

We do not intentionally collect analytics or tracking information about players ourselves.

The Game is developed using Unity. Depending on the version of the Game and the software components included in the Android build, certain technical information may potentially be processed by the Android operating system, Google Play, Unity, or other platform services as part of providing, securing, or operating those services.

We do not use this information to personally identify players or maintain player profiles.

Third-Party Services

Farm Harvest is distributed through Google Play and is developed using the Unity game engine.

Google Play and the Android operating system may process limited technical information in connection with downloading, installing, updating, securing, and operating applications. Such processing is governed by the relevant third party's own privacy policies and terms.

We do not intentionally send personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Farm Harvest is a family-friendly game with cute and cartoon-style graphics and no violence.

The Game does not require players to create an account or provide personal information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Farm Harvest.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not intentionally collect or maintain personal information from players, we do not maintain a database of player personal information that requires deletion.

If you believe that we have received personal information from you in connection with Farm Harvest, please contact us using the contact details below and we will review the request.

Security

We do not collect or maintain personal information through Farm Harvest. As a result, there is no player personal-information database maintained by us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updated version will be made available at the same privacy-policy URL. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when changes are made.
